What ScanDoc is

ScanDoc is a free, browser-based toolkit for OCR (text extraction from images), image-to-PDF conversion, text-to-PDF generation, PDF-to-image conversion, PDF-to-text extraction, and translation of extracted text. Processing runs locally in your browser wherever technically possible; translation is the exception, handled by a third-party API as described in our Privacy Policy.
