Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ScanDoc is built to process your documents without sending your files to a server. This page explains exactly what that means in practice, and where the few exceptions are.

What happens to your images and PDFs

Text extraction (OCR), image-to-PDF, text-to-PDF, PDF-to-image, and PDF-to-text conversion all run locally in your browser using JavaScript/WebAssembly libraries (Tesseract.js, PDF.js, jsPDF). Your images and documents are never uploaded to ScanDoc's servers, because ScanDoc does not operate a processing server — there isn't one to upload to. Files stay on your device and are cleared from memory when you close the tab or clear your results.

The one feature that does contact a server: Translation

When you use the "Translate Extracted Text" feature, the text you choose to translate is sent to a third-party free translation API (MyMemory Translated) so it can return a translation. This is the only feature in ScanDoc that transmits any of your content off your device. If you don't use translation, nothing is sent anywhere. MyMemory's own privacy practices are outside ScanDoc's control — see MyMemory's privacy policy for details on how they handle submitted text.

Analytics

ScanDoc uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. how many people visit, which pages are popular). This collects standard analytics data such as your browser, approximate location, and pages viewed — it does not have access to the images, PDFs, or text you process. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising

ScanDoc is free to use and is supported by ads served through Adsterra. Ad networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads; they do not have access to the content you process with ScanDoc's tools.

Cookies and local storage

ScanDoc may use your browser's local storage or cache to speed up repeat visits (for example, caching the OCR engine files so they don't re-download every time) and to support offline/PWA functionality. This data stays on your device.

Children's privacy

ScanDoc is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the contact details on the ScanDoc website.
